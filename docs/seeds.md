# Pick your seed customers

The list is only as good as the 3 to 5 customers you start from.

## Which customers

- **Pick the ones you would clone:** they bought fast, stayed, and pay a fair price. Not your biggest logo, not a friend's company.
- **Keep them alike on purpose.** Three software companies of 51 to 500 people give a tight search. One software company, one bank and one retailer give a search that matches everyone.
- **3 to 5 is enough.** More seeds add values, and every value widens the search.

## Where the fields come from

| Field | Where to read it |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn id | The number in `linkedin.com/company/<id>`, or in the company's Sales Navigator URL |
| Domain | The company website, without `www.` |
| Industry | The company's LinkedIn page, under the name. Use LinkedIn's wording exactly |
| Employees | LinkedIn's range: `11 to 50`, `51 to 200`, `201 to 500`, `501 to 1000`... |
| Location | The company headquarters, as LinkedIn writes it: `Paris, Île-de-France, France` |

If your CRM is connected to your AI Agent, it can read most of these from the company records. An Evaboot export of anyone at the company also holds all five.

## What the agent does with them

`lookalike.py profile` counts each value. Every value goes into the search: the industries and sizes as company filters, the regions as both the person's region and the company headquarters. A value only one customer has widens the list, so the agent asks before keeping it.
